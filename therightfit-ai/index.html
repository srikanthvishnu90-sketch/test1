Questions

Everything parents ask first.

Is it safe for my child?

Yes — safety is the core of the engine. Volume and intensity are capped by age band, recovery days are built in and can't be skipped, and the plan never programs adult workouts scaled down. It's general guidance, not medical advice — check with a physician before starting.

What ages and sports does it cover?

Built for ages 8–18, across basketball, soccer, tennis, baseball/softball, golf, volleyball, football, swimming, track, lacrosse and more — with sport- and position-specific drills.

How does the AI actually personalize it?

Your answers (and optional fitness tests) are turned into real capability scores. Drills are selected from a benchmark-backed library to target the athlete's specific weaknesses, then a coach's note explains the week. Re-test to earn a harder level.

Do we need special equipment?

No. The plan adapts to the space and gear you have — most drills need little more than a ball and an open area. You tell us what's available during setup.

What does it cost?

Start with a free 4-week plan. Membership is $14.99/mo (or $125/yr) for plans that adapt weekly, the AI coach, and parent reports — or grab a one-time full report for $29. Cancel anytime.
